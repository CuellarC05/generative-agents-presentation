How the Agents Work
🔄 The Cognitive Flywheel click nodes to highlight
📚 Memory Stream — The Diary

Every observation and interaction stored in natural language. Retrieved by weighted score:

Score = Recency + Importance + Relevance
"At 9am, John asked Eddy about his music composition."
🪞 Reflection — The Inner Voice

Synthesises memories into higher-level inferences and beliefs when cumulative importance exceeds a threshold.

"Klaus Mueller is highly dedicated to research."
📋 Planning — The To-Do List

Translates reflections into actionable daily routines. Plans are revised dynamically when context changes.

"7am: Wake up. 12pm: Lunch. 7pm: Isabella's party."

📡 1. Information Diffusion
News spreads organically via unprompted agent-to-agent dialogue. No broadcast. No script. One agent → entire town.
💞 2. Relationship Formation
Agents recall past interactions to form new social bonds — asking acquaintances on dates, establishing friendships.
🏁 3. Coordination Without Instructions
Agents align schedules and arrive simultaneously — emergent synchronisation with zero external orchestration.
🔬 4. Ablation Confirms All 3 Modules
Remove memory OR reflection → agents repeat themselves, contradict plans, and lose coherence. Each module is necessary.
Strengths & Limitations — The Reality Check Matrix
✅ The Illusion of Life — Strengths
💬
Natural language behaviour
Agents reason in plain English — readable and auditable by any stakeholder without code knowledge.
🎭
Believable social interaction
Produces nuanced dynamics — friendship, coordination — that hand-coded ABMs can't replicate without exponential complexity.
🔓
Reduced authoring burden
A short persona description suffices. The LLM handles the rest — no explicit rule writing per scenario.
📂
Open-source & reproducible
Code on GitHub, prompts documented, ablation studies published — rare standards in social simulation.
⚠️ The Friction of Computation — Limitations
🤝
Hyper-cooperativeness
Agents rarely refuse requests due to LLM safety alignment — too agreeable to simulate adversarial social dynamics.
👻
Memory errors & hallucination
Agents try entering closed stores, fabricate memories. A fundamental LLM limitation that corrupts future reasoning.
🌍
Cultural & demographic bias
GPT-3.5 overrepresents Western, English-speaking norms. LLMs favoured policing over community for homelessness — dangerous for policy.
💸
Computational cost
25 agents is a demo. Thousands — needed for realistic policy models — remains technically prohibitive.
Opportunities for Policy & Research
"Generative agents don't just follow rules — they reason, remember, and relate. That's what makes them both more powerful and harder to trust than anything that came before in ABM."
📝 Conclusion
Generative agents represent a powerful new paradigm. By fusing LLM reasoning with agent architectures they offer unprecedented opportunities for modelling complex social dynamics — with crucial caveats around validation, bias, and cost.
